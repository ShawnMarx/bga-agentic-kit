**Edit entries in place.** A rule that turns out wrong gets rewritten or deleted, never left next to
its replacement. Every entry carries a confidence tag and a date:

- `HYPOTHESIS` — derived from the primer's arithmetic, never tested at a table.
- `HOLDING` — seen to work, small sample.
- `SETTLED` — reliable across several games.
- `NARROWED` — was stated too broadly; now restricted to the case where it holds (say which).
- `WRONG` — kept only while it's a mistake still tempting to make; otherwise delete it.

**Every entry states its scope**: the board state it was learned in, and what it does *not* cover. A
narrow lesson remembered broadly makes play worse than before it was learned.

Entry shape:

```
**`SETTLED YYYY-MM-DD` — <the rule, one line>.**
<the case it came from; the user's words verbatim if it was a correction; what it does NOT cover>
```
